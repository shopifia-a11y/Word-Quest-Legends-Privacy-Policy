Word Quest Legends Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026.

This policy explains what information the Word Quest Legends app for Android ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by VantaSoft ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device. We don't receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (completed levels, coins, achievements, statistics, daily reward streak) and your settings (sound, vibration and theme) in the app's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google Account, so your progress can be restored on a new device. Google handles these backups under its own terms, and you can turn backup off in your device settings.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

  • Your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads may be personalized based on this information unless you opt out. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Ad personalization: you can manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Your game data: you can erase your progress at any time with Settings > Reset Game Progress in the app. Uninstalling the app also deletes it from your device.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data we share or sell

We don't sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data that the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google, the app doesn't share information with anyone.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads, and vibration for optional haptic feedback. It doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
